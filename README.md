## 💰 PayShare: The simplest way to share and repay expenses with crypto

PayShare is a Mini App available through Nimiq Pay that allows users to share expenses with friends and repay their share directly in cryptocurrency.

A restaurant, a weekend with friends, a trip, or a group gift: shared expenses are part of everyday life. However, even existing solutions often require multiple steps between creating an expense and actually completing the repayment.

PayShare simplifies the entire process by bringing expense sharing, calculation, and payment together in one seamless experience.

---

## 🚀 How does it work?

### 1. Create a group

Create a group for a specific occasion (weekend, trip, event…) and invite your friends instantly through a shareable link or QR code.

### 2. Add expenses

Add each expense with its name, amount, and select the participants involved. PayShare automatically calculates the split according to the chosen rules.

### 3. Repay directly in crypto

Each participant can instantly see how much they owe and pay their share directly through Nimiq Pay. The amount is automatically filled in: no manual input, no calculations, and no crypto addresses to copy.

### 4. Track repayments

Monitor expenses, completed payments, and remaining balances in real time until the group is fully settled.

## ✨ Main features

- Direct payments in NIM through Nimiq Pay
- Automatically calculated and pre-filled repayment amounts
- Interactive product tour guiding users through the app experience
- Creation of expense groups
- Invitation through shareable links or QR codes
- Adding expenses with amounts and participants
- Automatic expense splitting
- Equal sharing or fully customizable expense distribution
- Automatic calculation of who owes what
- Transaction optimization by automatically merging multiple payments to the same user
- Tracking of completed and pending repayments
- Expense and payment history
- Overview of amounts to pay and receive
- Internal notifications to track group activity

## 🌍 A crypto experience designed for everyday use

PayShare does not replace wallets: it gives them a practical use case.

Wallets already allow users to transfer value. PayShare adds an everyday experience by allowing users to share expenses and repay friends as easily as they would with a traditional payment app.

With its intuitive interface and built-in product tour, PayShare is designed to make crypto payments accessible even to users discovering this ecosystem for the first time.

Our goal is simple: remove technical complexity and make crypto payments feel natural—keeping only the essential action: paying a friend back.
