2. Purpose of PayShare

PayShare is an application designed to facilitate the management and sharing of expenses between several people.

The application notably allows users to:

  • create expense groups;
  • record and split expenses between multiple participants;
  • determine the amounts owed by each participant;
  • calculate balances between participants;
  • facilitate the settlement of amounts owed using crypto-assets;
  • generate and facilitate payments through the Nimiq Pay environment.

PayShare is primarily a tool for calculating, organizing and facilitating reimbursements between users.
